Radar Command: free 3D air traffic control game

Radar Command is a free air traffic control simulator you play in your browser, on desktop or mobile, with nothing to download. You are the controller of a busy airport: planes fly in from the edges of the map and it is up to you to guide each one down safely.

How to play

  • Drag from a plane to draw its flight path; it follows the path at a constant speed.
  • Land it by crossing the threshold of the runway of its colour in the direction of the runway arrow. If the runway is busy the plane goes around.
  • Watch the arrows on the screen edge: they warn where the next plane flies in.
  • New runways open as your score grows and more planes share the sky. One collision ends the shift.

Features

  • 3D airport with day and night, seasons, weather, clouds and river traffic
  • Departures, overflights, several aircraft types and live airport sounds
  • Online daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards
  • Mouse, touch and full keyboard controls; install it to your home screen

If you like Flight Control-style games, airport simulators or plane landing games, Radar Command is a modern take on the idea. The source code is open on GitHub.
