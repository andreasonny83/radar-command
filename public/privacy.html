Privacy Policy

Radar Command · web game and Android app · Last updated 30 October 2026

Radar Command is a free air traffic control game. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. This page explains what the game stores and sends, in plain terms. It applies to the web game at radar-command.vercel.app and to the Android app, which runs the same game.

The short version

  • You can play without giving us anything. The game works offline.
  • Only if you submit a score to the leaderboard do we receive a nickname and your run's score.
  • We never ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell your data and do not show advertising.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps these small items in your browser's or app's local storage. They never leave your device unless noted:

  • Your settings: sound and music on/off, hidden or moved panels.
  • The nickname you last used for the leaderboard (sent to us only when you submit a score).
  • A random anonymous player ID, which groups your own runs on the leaderboard. It is not linked to your identity or device.
  • The last leaderboard view, so it can show something while offline.
  • On the web, an offline copy of the game files (a service worker cache).

Clearing the site's data (or uninstalling the app) removes all of this.

What we collect when you use the leaderboard

Viewing the leaderboard, or starting a shift, contacts our server. Submitting a score stores one record containing:

  • the nickname you chose (3–16 characters). It is shown publicly on the leaderboard, so do not use your real name or anything personal;
  • your score and its breakdown (planes landed and departed, flight time, shift duration);
  • your anonymous player ID;
  • the time of the submission;
  • a keyed one-way hash of your IP address, used only to limit how fast one address can submit. We do not store the IP address itself, and the hash cannot be turned back into it.

Like any web server, our host also sees your IP address and device/browser details in the normal course of delivering a request, for operating and securing the service.

Nickname checking

To keep the leaderboard free of offensive names, a submitted nickname is checked on our server against a word list and, when enabled, by an AI model from TypeSafe to which only the nickname is sent (no player ID, IP address or other data). If a name is refused you can try another.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count visits and measure loading performance. They are designed to work without cookies and without following you across sites. They record things like the page, country, browser and device type, and performance timings. This data is aggregate and we do not use it to identify you.

Services we rely on

  • Vercel: hosting, the leaderboard API, analytics.
  • Neon: the database that stores leaderboard records.
  • TypeSafe: the optional nickname check described above.
  • GitHub: the "Send feedback" link opens a pre-filled issue form on GitHub. Nothing is sent unless you submit that form yourself, under GitHub's own policy.

These providers process data on our behalf, and may do so outside your country.

Android permissions

The Android app requests only the INTERNET permission, to reach the leaderboard and load analytics. It does not access your location, contacts, files, camera or microphone.

Retention and your choices

Leaderboard records are kept for as long as the leaderboard runs. You can stop sending us anything by not submitting a score. To have your records removed, open an issue at github.com/andreasonny83/radar-command/issues with your nickname and roughly when you played; because we hold no account or email, we can only match records by nickname, so the more detail the better.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not aimed at collecting data from children. We knowingly collect nothing beyond the optional nickname and score described above. A parent who wants a child's leaderboard entry removed can use the contact above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.

Contact
